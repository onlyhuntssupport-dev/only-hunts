# **App Name**: Only-Hunts

## Core Features:

- Outfitter Profile Management: Allows South African outfitters to create, update, and manage detailed profiles, including hunt packages, available dates, pricing, photos, and contact information.
- Hunter Profile & Preferences: Enables international hunters to register, create personal profiles, save their hunting preferences (e.g., target species, budget, experience level), and track their inquiries.
- Outfitter Search & Filtering: Provides a robust search interface for hunters to discover outfitters and hunt packages based on various criteria such as location, species, hunt type, and amenities.
- Inquiry & Communication System: Facilitates direct communication between hunters and outfitters through an integrated messaging system, allowing hunters to send inquiries and outfitters to respond.
- Outfitter Dashboard: Offers a personalized dashboard for outfitters to manage incoming inquiries, view booking requests, update their availability calendar, and monitor their listings.
- Hunting Trip Review & Rating: Allows hunters to submit reviews and assign ratings to outfitters after completing their trips, helping other hunters make informed decisions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Dark Olive Green (Professional, conservation-focused).
- Secondary Color: Kalahari Sand Brown (Warm, authentic, African landscape).
- Accent/Background: Off White (Clean, high-contrast readability).
- Theme: Rugged yet premium; high-end safari aesthetic.
